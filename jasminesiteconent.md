# Jasmine Learning

## A calm and organic learning center for children

**Holistic. Constructivist. Community-based.**

At **Jasmine Learning**, children learn through thoughtful questions, meaningful projects, hands-on discovery, and gentle guidance.

We are a calm and organic learning center for children aged **4 to 12**, where learners explore big ideas through Socratic discussions, creative expression, teamwork, and real-world challenges.

Our goal is simple:

> To make quality holistic and constructivist education accessible to more families at a reasonable price.

---

## Learning that nurtures the whole child

In today's world, children need more than worksheets, memorization, and pressure-driven learning. They need spaces where they can think deeply, ask questions, build confidence, work with their hands, express themselves, develop kindness and resilience, and learn at a natural pace.

Jasmine Learning is designed as a slow-paced, aesthetic, community-based center where children feel safe, seen, and inspired.

---

## What makes Jasmine Learning different?

### Socratic discussions

Children are encouraged to think, question, listen, and respond. Instead of being given all the answers, they learn how to explore ideas through guided discussion.

### Hands-on, project-based learning

Learning journeys include practical, creative, and meaningful projects that children can build, present, and reflect on.

### A calm, organic environment

The setting is intentionally warm, calm, and aesthetic because environment shapes how children feel and learn.

### Community-based learning

Children collaborate, share ideas, support one another, and become part of a kind learning community.

## Our learning approach

At Jasmine Learning, we follow a **holistic constructivist approach**. Children actively build understanding through experience, conversation, observation, creativity, and reflection.

Our programs may include:

- Socratic discussions
- Story-based learning
- Hands-on projects
- Art and creative expression
- Team missions
- Research-based challenges
- Reflection circles
- Exhibitions and presentations
- Real-world problem-solving
- Emotional and social development activities

The aim is to help children become more thoughtful, confident, kind, and capable.

# What families can expect

## What we do promise

- A meaningful learning process
- Hands-on learning
- A safe space for mistakes
- Collaboration and connection to community
- Intrinsic motivation and critical thinking
- Real-world connection
- Self-regulation and creativity
- Facilitation, not lectures
- Personalized pathways

## What we don't promise

- Rote memorization
- Perfect handwriting
- One right way to learn
- Instant results

Jasmine Learning is a learning community—not a tuition center or test-preparation program. We facilitate children as they think, create, reflect, and grow in a calm, non-pressurized environment.

# Our philosophy

## The sun is not only for a few

At Jasmine Learning, we believe meaningful education should not belong only to the privileged. Knowledge, care, creativity, and emotional nourishment should be shared more generously.

A candle was never meant to be hidden. And the sun was never meant to belong to one person.

**Jasmine Learning exists to create a warm, thoughtful, and accessible space where more children can grow in light.**
